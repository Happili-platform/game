# Vision & Mission

## Our Vision

**Open and unified AI agent management for every enterprise.**

To enable organizations to manage autonomous AI agents with transparency, accountability, and independence across cloud, SaaS, and private environments.

## Our Mission

**Build an open-source, vendor-neutral management layer for autonomous AI.**

Empower developers and enterprises to collaboratively discover, govern, observe, and financially manage AI agents through an extensible, interoperable platform.

## Our Community Principle

**Built in the open. Improved by the community. Adaptable to every enterprise.**

We believe enterprise AI agent management should benefit from open collaboration, transparent architecture, and shared innovation.

Our community welcomes contributions from developers, enterprise architects, AI engineers, security practitioners, FinOps specialists, and researchers.

Together, we aim to:

- Expand integration across cloud, SaaS, and private AI agent environments.
- Strengthen agent governance, security, and accountability.
- Advance runtime observability and Agentic FinOps.
- Improve enterprise SaaS and MCP governance.
- Build reliable, extensible, and well-documented open-source software.

## Our Commitment

GAMe Community is an evolving open-source initiative.

We are committed to responsible development, transparent collaboration, constructive technical discussion, and clear documentation of both capabilities and limitations.

We welcome new ideas, architectural feedback, testing, documentation improvements, and code contributions.
